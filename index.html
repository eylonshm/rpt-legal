RPT Replies

RPT Replies is the private tool the Real Plot Twists channel uses to answer comments on its own videos on Instagram, YouTube and TikTok.

It reads new comments on videos the channel posted, drafts a short reply, and posts the reply from the channel's own account, usually after the channel owner approves it. It only works with the channel's own accounts. It does not post on anyone else's content, send direct messages, or collect data for advertising.
