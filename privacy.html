Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

RPT Replies is a private tool used by the Real Plot Twists channel to reply to comments on its own videos on Instagram, YouTube and TikTok. This policy explains what it handles.

What it accesses

  • The Real Plot Twists channel's own accounts, with permissions the channel owner granted: its list of posted videos, the comments on those videos, and the ability to post replies.
  • For each comment: the comment text, the commenter's public username, and the time it was posted.

It does not access other people's accounts, private messages, contact lists, email addresses or payment details.

How it is used

Comment text is used only to draft and post a reply to that comment. To draft a reply, the comment text and details about the video are sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which does not use API data to train its models. Data is never sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

Storage and deletion

Comments and replies are stored on the channel's private server and deleted automatically after 60 days. Access tokens are kept on that server in files only the channel owner's account can read, and can be revoked at any time in the platform's settings.

Removing your data

If you commented on a Real Plot Twists video and want your comment data removed from this tool, delete your comment on the platform or contact us, and it will be removed within 7 days.

Platform policies

Use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is handled under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
